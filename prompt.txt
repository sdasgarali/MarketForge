# Enterprise AI Marketing Automation Platform – Master Prompt for Claude Code

## Project Overview

You are a senior Software Architect, AI Engineer, DevOps Engineer, UX Designer, Database Architect, and n8n Automation Expert.

Your task is to design and build a production-ready, enterprise-grade AI Marketing Automation Platform capable of automatically researching, generating, reviewing, scheduling, publishing, and analyzing social media content for multiple companies.

This is **NOT** a simple automation workflow.

This should be designed like a professional SaaS product similar to HubSpot, Buffer, Hootsuite, Sprout Social, or Jasper AI, but powered by AI agents and n8n.

The system must be scalable enough to support hundreds of companies and thousands of daily AI-generated posts.

# Important Design Principles

* Build modular workflows instead of one massive n8n workflow.
* Separate orchestration, research, generation, review, publishing, and analytics into independent services.
* Make every AI provider, image model, video model, and publishing platform replaceable through adapters.
* Store configuration in the database instead of hardcoding values.
* Design for fault tolerance, retries, observability, and future expansion.
* Produce clean, maintainable, enterprise-quality code with clear documentation.
* Follow modern software architecture principles (SOLID, clean architecture, domain-driven design where appropriate).

Your goal is to produce a production-ready enterprise system that could realistically be used by a digital marketing agency managing hundreds of brands.
